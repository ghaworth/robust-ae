Current client work:

- Vertical farming control panels for Square Mile Farms.

Two technical tracks running alongside the consultancy:

- Chartered Engineer application through the IET experiential pathway. Writing up a decade of embedded and industrial controls work against the UK-SPEC competences.
- An open-source BLDC motor driver Pmod for the ULX3S FPGA board. Two halves: field-oriented control gateware in the open ECP5 flow (Yosys and nextpnr), benchmarked against a commercial Trenz TEP0002 driver board, and an independent open-hardware driver Pmod released for anyone to use.
